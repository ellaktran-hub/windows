privacy.txt

Home Window is a personal planner used by one person, its owner.

It reads the owner's Notion planner and, with permission, today's events from the owner's Google Calendar. Calendar access is read only. It never changes, adds, or deletes calendar events.

It does not store a copy of this data, does not sell or share it, and does not use it for advertising. Data is shown only to the signed-in owner.

The owner can remove calendar access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
